The smarter way to choose where to buy or rent

Traditional property search sites make it notoriously hard to balance two or more commutes. MapToucher solves the two-commuter puzzle before you browse listings.

❌

The Portal Search Struggle

  • × Single-location bias: Portals force you to search within a radius of one postcode, neglecting your partner's or housemates' commutes.
  • × Crude radius circles: As-the-crow-flies circles ignore winding roads, rivers, bottlenecks, and real highway access.
  • × Endless tab switching: Checking commute durations manually on Google Maps for every single listing wastes hours.
✨

The MapToucher Approach

  • ✓ Simultaneous commute limits: Add up to 6 locations (e.g. Work A, Work B, School) with customized travel time budgets.
  • ✓ True road-network isochrones: Calculates genuine driving and walking contours based on actual road networks and speed limits.
  • ✓ The Golden Zone: Pinpoints the exact overlapping area where you should draw your search on Rightmove, Zoopla, or Zillow.
