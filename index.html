Find where everyone can meet.

Whether planning a meetup with friends, coordinating a team gathering, or house hunting together, MapToucher calculates realistic driving and walking travel times to find the fair middle ground.

Launch MapToucher View on GitHub

How it works

Three simple steps to find your shared sweet spot.

1

Add starting points

Enter addresses, postcodes, or landmarks for everyone in the group. Instant autocomplete helps you pick the right spot fast.

2

Set travel limits

Choose how far each person is willing to travel (up to 60 minutes) and their mode of travel — drive or walk.

3

See the overlap

MapToucher calculates actual road-network isochrones and highlights the golden zone reachable from every single location.

Multi-Place Overlap
